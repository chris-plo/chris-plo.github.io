# A Newsletter Needs More Than a Subscribe Button

I began with a practical goal: make a website that explains my work clearly. As I kept revising it, I found myself asking a different question: would someone arriving here find something useful, and would it feel like it came from me?

That question shaped a lot of small decisions. I moved things around, rewrote parts that sounded too much like a CV, and kept adjusting until the work, the writing and the personal details felt at home together. I wanted the site to help someone understand what I do, while still leaving room for the things I’m curious about and the way I think.

Adding the newsletter made the site feel different to me. The form is small, but it gives readers a choice: if something here interests them, they can ask to hear when I write again. I like that the connection is simple and on their terms.

It also gives me a reason to keep building. The site isn’t a finished presentation I put online and walk away from. I can add an idea, improve something that isn’t working, and send a note when there’s something new to share. That little button is an invitation to follow along.
